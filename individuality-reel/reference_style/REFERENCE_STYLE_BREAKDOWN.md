## Style rules extracted
- **Presenter:** one locked medium shot the whole time, no punch-ins, natural colour, head top ≈ y 660.
- **B-roll:** landscape photos/stills, ~700–780 px wide, centred, top ≈ 220–290, above the head; no border, no shadow, no motion; hard cuts; ~3 s each, every ~6–7 s, face never covered.
- **Type:** yellow serif headline only for the hook; captions white, bold sans ~44 px, 1–2 lines, sitting on the chest (y ≈1250–1310), no karaoke/no colour highlights.
- **Transitions:** none beyond hard cuts.
- **Music:** quiet warm tonal bed (low register, ~117 BPM feel) 15–25 dB under voice; no SFX, no whooshes.
