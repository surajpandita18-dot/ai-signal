## 4. Audio/video sync analysis (the honest version)

The clean audio and the camera take are **two different performances**. Different durations (98.4 s vs 88.5 s), different repeats and stumbles, and different phrase timing (for example "Look around … cafés": 6.5 s clean vs 5.8 s camera). A word-accurate lip sync of the clean audio onto the camera footage is **not possible**. Stretching video per phrase would drift 100–300 ms inside sentences, which is visible on a close-up face.

| Option | How | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Hybrid by sentence (recommended)** | Sentences shown on camera use the camera's own audio (cleaned and EQ/loudness-matched to the clean mic). Sentences covered by B-roll, quote cards or graphics use the clean audio. Switches happen only at sentence boundaries. | Perfect lip sync on your face; best audio elsewhere; your face stays central. | Two mics. I match tone and level, but a careful listener may hear a slight change at some switches. |
| B. Clean audio only | Clean voice throughout; your face only where a phrase matches within ~3%; the rest covered. | One consistent voice. | Your face is on screen much less; mild lip drift risk. |
| C. Camera audio only | Camera performance throughout (cleaned); clean audio only to patch the camera's flaws under B-roll. | Simplest, perfectly in sync, one mic. | Slightly noisier voice (SNR 28 vs 31 dB, both fine after cleanup). |
