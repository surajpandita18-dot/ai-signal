AI can think for you. But should it?

We've always handed thinking to tools. Socrates worried writing would weaken memory. Teachers fought calculators. Psychologists call it cognitive offloading, and most of the time it's a gift.

But generative AI doesn't just store or calculate. It drafts the reasoning. A 2025 Microsoft Research study of 319 knowledge workers found that in most of their 936 real examples, critical thinking felt like less effort with AI, and the more people trusted the AI, the less critical thinking they reported.

The twist: the thinking didn't disappear. It moved, from finding answers to checking them, from doing the task to overseeing it. That's a skill too, if we keep practising it.

So, which kind of user are you: substitute or thinking partner?
