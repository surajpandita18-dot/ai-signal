- Original article: Maria Popova, "The Overview Effect and the Psychology of Cosmic Awe", *The Marginalian*, 18 Dec 2012 — https://www.themarginalian.org/index.php/2012/12/18/the-overview-effect-and-the-psychology-of-cosmic-awe/
- Frank White, *The Overview Effect: Space Exploration and Human Evolution* (1987). "A cognitive shift in awareness."
- Apollo 8 onboard recording, transcript by NASA Goddard Scientific Visualization Studio ("Earthrise", 2018) — https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004500/a004593/G2018_Earthrise_Master_Output.en_US.srt ; story: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/the-story-behind-apollo-8s-famous-earthrise-photo/

- "Earthrise is often credited with helping spark the environmental movement" is a widely made claim, phrased as such.
