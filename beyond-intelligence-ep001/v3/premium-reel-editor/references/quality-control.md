# Final Quality-Control Checklist

## Story
- [ ] First 1–2 seconds establish a clear reason to watch.
- [ ] Hook is truthful and paid off.
- [ ] No confusing jumps in logic or chronology.
- [ ] Every section earns its duration.
- [ ] Ending feels intentional.

## Picture
- [ ] Correct aspect ratio and framing.
- [ ] No accidental black frames, freezes, or duplicate frames.
- [ ] No face/object obscured by captions.
- [ ] B-roll is relevant and factually appropriate.
- [ ] Graphics are consistent and typo-free.
- [ ] Skin tones and exposure are natural.
- [ ] No distracting transitions or unnecessary zooms.

## Captions
- [ ] Word-for-word checked against audio.
- [ ] Hinglish spelling and script convention consistent.
- [ ] Proper nouns and technical terms verified.
- [ ] Captions synchronised and readable.
- [ ] Safe margins respected.

## Speech edits
- [ ] Every inner-sentence cut sits at ≤ −40 dBFS with 20 ms fades.
- [ ] Every inner-sentence splice re-transcribed and compared with the uncut original.
- [ ] Hesitations inside names or phrases joined; other inner pauses not shorter than ~0.20 s.
- [ ] No repeated syllables or words; caption word count equals the transcript word count.

## Sound
- [ ] Dialogue clear and dominant.
- [ ] No clipping, pops, clicks, or abrupt edits.
- [ ] Music ducked under speech.
- [ ] SFX are purposeful and not excessive.
- [ ] Audio sync checked throughout.

## Rights and evidence
- [ ] Asset log complete.
- [ ] Source and license recorded for external assets.
- [ ] No unverified quotations, fake screenshots, or misleading visuals.
- [ ] AI-generated illustrative visuals are labelled when necessary.

## Technical export
- [ ] MP4 container
- [ ] H.264 video
- [ ] 1080 × 1920 target (or explicitly approved alternative)
- [ ] 9:16 aspect ratio
- [ ] Intended frame rate
- [ ] AAC audio present
- [ ] Duration matches target
- [ ] Full export watched/listened to
- [ ] Full-file automated pass: blackdetect, freezedetect, silencedetect, peak/clipping, A/V duration match
- [ ] Contact sheet at 1 frame per second reviewed
- [ ] Final audio re-transcribed and compared with the script
- [ ] Creator told to review the downloaded original, not a chat-app preview

## Report statuses honestly
Use `PASS`, `WARN`, `FAIL`, or `NOT CHECKED`. Automated metadata checks do not replace full playback review.
