# Permanent Reel Style Guide: Suraj Pandita

Applies to every Reel. Updated after the Beyond Intelligence Ep 001 (AlphaGo) edit, v3.

## North star
Premium documentary + modern creator + cinematic video essay. Story first, effects second. Fewer effects and better storytelling beat a busy edit.

## Structure
1. **Cold open (0–10 s):** the strongest truthful moment of the story, in your own words. The frame-0 visual and on-screen line say the same thing as the first spoken line.
2. **Brand sting (2–3 s)** right after the hook. Never a long animated intro.
3. **Context → escalation → reveal → human twist → reflection.** Every section earns its time.
4. **Ending:** the reflective line lands on your face with a slow push-in and no graphics, then a 2.5–3 s end card. No "follow for more" unless asked.

## Pacing and speech
- Remove dead air, false starts and repeated takes. Keep breathing room.
- Hesitations inside a name or phrase are joined. Other inner pauses are about 0.20 s. Deliberate beats stay as recorded: before a reveal, after a big number, before the final question.
- Speed: up to 1.10× with pitch preserved, only when the topic is dense. Never faster.
- Cuts at quiet points with 20 ms fades. Every inner-sentence cut is re-checked.

## Picture
- Talking head is the anchor, not the whole show. About 40–55% motivated visuals for explainers.
- Two base framings (wide and medium). Switch only on take changes. One tight emphasis framing for 3–4 pivotal lines. Slow push-ins only on the hook and the closing thought.
- Visuals in priority order: authentic photos/footage → detail crops of them → accurate diagrams → clean original graphics (labelled "Illustration"). No random stock footage.
- Callbacks: return to an earlier key visual when the narration refers back to it.

## Colour
- Natural skin, gentle contrast, light vignette. No crushed blacks, no heavy teal-orange.
- Archival photos are graded softly to sit with the footage (slight desaturation, warm neutral).

## Graphics and typography
- **Beyond Intelligence default:** charcoal #121110, off-white #EDE8DE, amber accent #D99A3D, Inter (300/600/700/800).
- **Other series or topics:** keep the same restraint (one accent, 2–3 weights, simple grid). The palette and texture can change to fit the subject.
- One big hand-set number per Reel when the story has one. Always add a source tag.

## Captions
- Hinglish in Roman script, exactly as spoken (script spelling for names and terms).
- Phrase chunks of up to 3 words. The spoken word turns yellow (#FFC83D) and key terms are yellow capitals. A small pop-in per phrase. Inter ExtraBold about 70 px with a soft outline.
- Placed in the lower third above the Instagram UI. Never over the face, the brand sting or the end card.
- SRT delivered with every Reel.

## Sound
- Voice first: about 22 dB above the music while speaking. Integrated −14 LUFS, peak about −1.5 dBTP.
- Original or properly licensed music only. Suspense layers (heartbeat, drone, rising tone) at most 4–5 times, each with a hard stop before the payoff.
- SFX only for story events: a stone click for a board or result, one subtle impact for the central reveal, a soft chime for the big number.

## Character / mascot (optional)
Small, on-brand, at most 3 appearances, only where the subject is "the machine". Never during the final thought.

## Delivery and QC
- 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, H.264 (CRF 16–18), AAC 256 kbps, +faststart.
- Full-file QC: black frames, freezes, silence, clipping, A/V sync, a contact sheet at 1 fps, and a full re-transcription compared with the script.
- Judge quality on the downloaded original, not a chat preview.
