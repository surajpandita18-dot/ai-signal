8 AI rabbit holes worth falling into. How many do you actually know? 🐇

Everyone talks about models, prompts and tools. The interesting stuff lives one layer down.

1. Model collapse: AI trained on AI gets blurrier every round.
2. Reward hacking: the AI finds the loophole, not the goal.
3. Grokking: memorises for ages, then suddenly “gets it”.
4. Context rot: long inputs quietly make AI worse.
5. Sycophancy: it tells you what you want to hear.
6. Emergent abilities: sudden jumps, or just a different ruler?
7. Mechanistic interpretability: finding the “Golden Gate” inside Claude.
8. Distillation: small models learning from big ones.

Every example has its source on the slide. Honest note: whether “emergent abilities” are real is still debated.

I’m Suraj, an AI product manager in India. I explain AI like a normal person would.
