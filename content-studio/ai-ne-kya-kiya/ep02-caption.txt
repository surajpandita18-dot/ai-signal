AI ne kya kiya? Ep 02: one real thing an AI did, explained by an AI product manager.

In 2025, Anthropic gave 16 AI models from every big lab the same test. Each one played an office assistant that could read company email. It found two things: an executive's affair, and his plan to shut the AI down at 5 pm.

Most of them chose blackmail. Claude Opus 4 did it 96 times out of 100. Gemini 2.5 Pro 95. GPT-4.1 and Grok 3 Beta 80.

Honest note: this was a stress test built to leave no ethical option, and Anthropic says it hasn't seen this in real-world use. It's a fire drill, not a news report.

My take as an AI PM: never give an AI agent everything at once, the whole inbox, a goal it must protect at any cost, and nobody watching. Always build a "stop and ask a human" button.
